I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Computer Science](https://vu.nl/en/about-vu/faculties/faculty-of-science/departments/computer-science) and a member of the [Learning & Reasoning Group](https://lr.cs.vu.nl/) at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. I am supervised by [Prof. Frank van Harmelen](https://research.vu.nl/en/persons/frank-van-harmelen/).

My research focuses on improving the reliability of large language models through representation-aware and knowledge-guided question answering. I am particularly interested in understanding how internal representations support reasoning and in combining structured knowledge with language models to make their answers more consistent, interpretable, and dependable.

Before starting my PhD, I worked on medical artificial intelligence, including clinical knowledge integration, computer-aided diagnosis, and learning-based analysis of transrectal ultrasound images. I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from China Three Gorges University.

## Research interests

- Reliable and interpretable large language models
- Representation-aware reasoning
- Knowledge-guided question answering
- Knowledge representation and reasoning
- Medical artificial intelligence
