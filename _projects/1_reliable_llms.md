My PhD research investigates how internal representations and structured knowledge can improve the reliability of large language models. The project focuses on representation-aware analysis, knowledge-guided question answering, and methods for making model behavior more consistent, interpretable, and dependable.

This work is carried out in the [Learning & Reasoning Group](https://lr.cs.vu.nl/) at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam under the supervision of [Prof. Frank van Harmelen](https://research.vu.nl/en/persons/frank-van-harmelen/).
