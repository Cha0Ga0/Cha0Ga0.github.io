This project developed accurate and efficient methods for prostate segmentation in transrectal ultrasound (TRUS) images. It explored supervised, semi-supervised, and knowledge-informed approaches to address limited annotations, noisy boundaries, and the reliability requirements of computer-aided diagnosis and image-guided procedures.

The research was conducted with the Urology Department of Yichang Central People's Hospital during my master's studies at China Three Gorges University.
